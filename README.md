# 🍕 Pizza Sales Performance Report 

An interactive **Pizza Sales Performance Dashboard** built using **Power BI** to analyze sales performance, customer ordering patterns, product popularity, and revenue trends. The dashboard provides actionable insights that help businesses optimize product offerings and improve sales performance.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project transforms pizza sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive Power BI visualizations. It enables users to monitor key sales KPIs, identify best- and worst-performing products, analyze customer ordering behavior, and evaluate revenue distribution across categories and pizza sizes.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Analyze revenue and order trends
- Identify top and bottom performing pizzas
- Understand customer ordering patterns
- Support data-driven business decisions

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- Executive KPI Cards
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Orders by Day of Week
- Pizza Category Analysis
- Pizza Size Analysis
- Revenue by Pizza Name
- Top & Bottom Performing Pizzas
- Interactive Slicers for Dynamic Filtering

## 📌 Key Insight

- Generated **$817.61K** in total revenue from **21,334 customer orders**.
- Sold **49,558 pizzas**, with customers purchasing an average of **2.32 pizzas per order**.
- The **Classic Deluxe Pizza** received the highest number of orders and quantity sold.
- **The Thai Chicken Pizza** generated the highest total revenue.
- **The Brie Carre Pizza** recorded the lowest revenue, orders, and quantity sold.
- **Large pizzas** contributed the largest share of total sales (**45.89%**).
- **Classic pizzas** generated the highest revenue contribution among all categories.
- Customer demand peaked on **Thursday**, while **Saturday** recorded the fewest orders.
- Revenue remained relatively stable throughout the year, with noticeable peaks during **July** and **November**.

## 💡 Data-Driven Recommendations

- Maintain adequate inventory for **Classic Deluxe** and **Thai Chicken** pizzas to meet customer demand.
- Review pricing, promotions, or recipe improvements for **Brie Carre Pizza** to improve sales.
- Increase staffing and inventory on **Thursdays**, when order volume is highest.
- Promote **Medium** and **Small** pizzas through combo offers to improve their contribution.
- Create targeted promotions during lower-performing months to stabilize monthly revenue.
- Bundle lower-selling pizzas with best-selling items to increase product visibility and sales.
- Continue monitoring sales by category and pizza size to optimize inventory planning and reduce waste.
